# Interview brief

## One-sentence pitch
I build scientific pipelines that connect numerical models and machine learning to operational evidence: tests, injection-recovery, quality gates, provenance, restartability and human-auditable products.

## Why two tracks in one repository?
The vacancy requires concurrent support for TOLIMAN and bolide detection. The shared engineering problem is not the science target; it is how to make subtle-signal and rare-event pipelines testable, calibrated, reviewable and releasable.

## Honest boundaries
- No direct TOLIMAN or GOES GLM mission-data result is claimed.
- The astrometric units and light curves are illustrative synthetic inputs.
- Direct Earth-observation, meteor and production-cloud experience remain learning areas.
- Existing evidence for operations and HPC comes from radio-pipeline-control-lab, Slurm workflows and survey data products.

## First 90 days
1. Reproduce accepted baselines and map data/quality contracts.
2. Create deterministic reference subsets and formal V&V tests.
3. Profile bottlenecks and improve algorithms only against agreed metrics.
4. Build candidate and quality reports that keep raw evidence accessible.
5. Document as-built algorithms, integration assumptions and release decisions.
