\textbf{Job-specific mission-pipeline preparation.}
To make my fit for the two mission tracks inspectable, I developed
\href{https://github.com/pkd-45/space-mission-pipeline-vv-lab}{\texttt{space-mission-pipeline-vv-lab}},
a controlled synthetic benchmark combining differential-astrometry
injection--recovery with grouped rare-event light-curve classification.
It records nuisance variables, compares null and signal models, measures
completeness and amplitude bias, uses class-balanced and calibration metrics,
prevents operational-group leakage, writes human-review candidate rankings,
and hashes release products behind explicit V\&V gates. The benchmark is not
TOLIMAN or GOES GLM mission software; it demonstrates the validation, data
contract and release discipline I would bring before working with mission data.
